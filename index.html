How I work

My strongest skill is understanding both sides of a game: the engineering underneath and the design on top. I can take a concept, find the hard technical problems in it, and design a foundation that the rest of the team can build on with confidence.

I've led development on most of my projects. That means making the architectural calls, coordinating contributors, prioritising features, reviewing code, and turning a creative vision into something that can actually be built.
